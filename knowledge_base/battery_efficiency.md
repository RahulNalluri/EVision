# EV Battery Efficiency Knowledge

Electric vehicle range is affected by battery state, vehicle speed, acceleration style, braking behavior, cabin climate load, terrain, traffic, and weather. A driver can improve practical range without increasing battery capacity by reducing avoidable energy losses.

Smooth acceleration reduces peak motor demand. In stop-go traffic, sudden starts consume more energy than gradual launches. Regenerative braking works best when the driver lifts early and allows the vehicle to recover energy before using friction brakes.

High speed increases aerodynamic drag. On many routes, a moderate and steady speed is more efficient than repeatedly accelerating and braking. Eco mode usually softens throttle response and reduces unnecessary energy demand.

Cabin cooling or heating can create a meaningful auxiliary load. A moderate cabin temperature, such as around 24C in warm conditions, can reduce climate energy use while keeping the driver comfortable.
